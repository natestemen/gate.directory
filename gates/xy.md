The XY gate is the one-parameter family generated by the exchange (hopping) interaction $XX + YY$. It is a *partial iSWAP*: it rotates within the $\{|01\rangle, |10\rangle\}$ subspace and leaves $|00\rangle$ and $|11\rangle$ untouched.

$$
\mathrm{XY}(\theta) = \exp\left(i \frac{\theta}{4} (X \otimes X + Y \otimes Y)\right)
$$

In the computational basis:

$$
\begin{bmatrix}
  1 & 0                      & 0                      & 0 \\
  0 & \cos\tfrac{\theta}{2}  & i\sin\tfrac{\theta}{2} & 0 \\
  0 & i\sin\tfrac{\theta}{2} & \cos\tfrac{\theta}{2}  & 0 \\
  0 & 0                      & 0                      & 1
\end{bmatrix}
$$

### Special values

| $\theta$ | Gate |
| --- | --- |
| $\pi$ | [iSWAP](/gates/iswap) |
| $\pi/2$ | [$\sqrt{i\mathrm{SWAP}}$](/gates/sqrt-iswap) |
| $2\pi$ | $Z \otimes Z$ |

### Properties

- Number-preserving: it commutes with the total excitation number, like [Givens rotations](/gates/givens) and [FSim](/gates/fsim). In fact $\mathrm{FSim}(\theta, 0) = \mathrm{XY}(-2\theta)$.
- Forms a one-parameter group: $\mathrm{XY}(\alpha) \, \mathrm{XY}(\beta) = \mathrm{XY}(\alpha + \beta)$.
- The complementary rotation on the even-parity subspace is the [$XX - YY$ gate](/gates/xx-minus-yy); the two commute, and together they compose $R_{xx}$ and $R_{yy}$.
- Beware conventions: Rigetti, Braket, and PennyLane use the matrix above, while Qiskit's `XXPlusYYGate` rotates with the opposite sign and adds a phase parameter $\beta$.

### Usage

- Native entangling gate on Rigetti hardware (as a calibrated pulse family), and a natural primitive for fermionic and hard-core boson simulation, where hopping terms are exactly of this form.

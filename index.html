    Missing a gate, or spotted a mistake? Open an issue or edit the page on GitHub; every page links to its source.

    Everything here is machine-readable too: all gates as JSON, one file per gate under /api/gates/, and the groups. Each record has the symbol and notations in LaTeX, the matrix as expressions in the gate's parameters, and the basic facts.
